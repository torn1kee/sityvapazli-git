2. რა მონაცემებს ვაგროვებთ

აპლიკაცია იყენებს Google-ით ავტორიზაციას (Google Sign-in) და Cloud ბაზას (Firebase). როდესაც თქვენ აპლიკაციაში შედიხართ:

  • თქვენი თამაშის პროგრესი (მონეტები) ინახება Firebase სერვერზე.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and process your information in the "სიტყვა ფაზლი" (Georgian Wordscapes) mobile app.

2. Data We Collect

Our app uses Google Sign-in and Firebase Firestore (Cloud Database). When you log in:

  • We collect and store your Email address and basic profile information from your Google Account.
  • We save your game progress (such as coins and current levels) securely on Firebase servers.

Additionally, we use third-party services (Google AdMob) which automatically collect technical and usage data, including:

  • Device Advertising ID.
  • Device type, operating system version, and general network information.
  • App usage data (crash logs, performance analytics).

3. How We Use the Data

The collected data is exclusively used for:

  • Storing your game progress safely in the cloud so you can restore it on different devices.
  • Delivering personalized and non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Improving app performance and fixing bugs or crashes.

4. Data Deletion Request (Right to Erasure)

If you wish to request the deletion of your account and associated data (game progress, coins, and your email address), please contact us directly at tookhachidze@gmail.com. Upon receiving your email, we will permanently delete all your data from our database securely.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at: tookhachidze@gmail.com.
